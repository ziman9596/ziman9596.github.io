IGC Samundri System

Ek fee app se shuru hua, ab poora college system ban raha hai.

Ek accountant, ek Excel sheet, aur entries jo kabhi skip ho jati thin. Wahan se maine fee app banayi, aur phir har naya hissa tab aaya jab college ke kisi aur department ko usi tarah ki problem thi. Yeh project abhi bhi barh raha hai, isliye neeche har cheez ka asli status likha hai.

Student portal: bara milestone

Students apna fee status aur attendance khud dekh sakte hain, aur yeh portal seedha app se connected hai. Yahan se system sirf accountant ka tool nahi raha, college ke har student tak pahunchta hai.

Live

  • Desktop appFees, vouchers jo print ke baad lock ho jate hain, har payment par automatic timestamp, offline chalta hai aur backup leta hai.
  • Website with admin panelCollege ki public website, aur admin panel se uska content manage hota hai.
  • Student portalFee status aur attendance, app se directly connected.
  • Teacher attendance portalEk jagah se saari classes ki attendance mark hoti hai.
  • Principal portalApp mein financial cheezein badalne se pehle principal ki approval.

Planned

  • Examination Department portalAlag tests ke marks upload karne ke liye.
  • Principal read-only viewPrincipal accountant ka sab kaam dekh sake, bina kuch badle.
  • Teacher portals aur unka adminTeachers apni attendance, timetable aur classes dekh saken, aur principal bhi unhe dekh sake.
  • Student uploadsStudents apni picture aur matric ki info khud upload karen, taake board registration ke waqt kaam kam ho.
  • Dashboard graphsAur zyada graphs aur summaries.
  • AI-based e-marking toolBoards bhi e-marking ki taraf ja rahe hain, isliye iska plan hai.

Design decisions

Vouchers lock after print

Chhapne ke baad koi unhe chupke se badal nahi sakta.

Manual log, automatic timestamp

Accountant ka kaam wahi rehta hai, bas ghalti ka mauqa kam.

Approval before financial changes

Paisay se jura koi badlav principal ki manzoori ke baghair nahi hota.

Offline-first, with backup

Bijli ya internet jaaye, kaam nahi rukta.

Yeh payment system nahi, management tool hai. Paisa pehle jaise handle hota hai, waise hi hota hai.
