IGC Samundri System

It began as a fee app and is growing into a complete college system.

One accountant, one Excel sheet, and entries that kept getting skipped. I built the fee app to fix that, and each new piece followed when another department had the same kind of problem. The project is still growing, so every item below carries its real status.

Student portal: a major milestone

Students can check their own fee status and attendance, and the portal is connected directly to the app. That takes the system beyond the accountant’s desk and puts it in front of every student.

Design decisions

Vouchers lock after print

Once printed, nobody can quietly change them.

Manual log, automatic timestamp

The accountant’s job stays the same, with less room for error.

Approval before financial changes

No money-related change happens without the principal’s approval.

Offline-first, with backup

When the power or internet drops, work continues.

This is a management tool, not a payment system. Money is handled exactly as before.
