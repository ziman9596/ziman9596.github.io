How I work

  1. Listen first

    I start by talking to the people who do the work. For IGC I worked in the accounts office first, which is how I found the real problem.

  2. Design around people

    A new system gets used when it fits how people already work instead of fighting it. Mine doesn’t change the accountant’s job. It just leaves less room for error.

  3. Build with AI, own the decisions

    AI does much of the heavy coding. Requirements, system design and what is worth building are my decisions.

  4. Protect the data

    Fee vouchers lock after printing, every payment is timestamped, and the system runs offline so a power or internet cut never stops the work.
