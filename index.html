How I work

  1. Listen first

    Main pehle us jagah ke log se baat karti hun jo kaam karte hain. IGC ke liye maine accounts office mein kaam kiya, tab samajh aaya asli problem kya hai.

  2. Design around people

    Naya system tab chalta hai jab woh log ke kaam karne ke tareeqe mein fit ho, usse ladne ke bajaye. Mera system accountant ka kaam nahi badalta, bas ghalti ka mauqa kam karta hai.

  3. Build with AI, own the decisions

    AI heavy coding karta hai. Requirements, system design aur kya banana hai, yeh faisle mere hote hain.

  4. Protect the data

    Fee vouchers print ke baad lock, har payment ka timestamp, aur system offline bhi chalta hai taake bijli ya internet jaane se kaam na ruke.
