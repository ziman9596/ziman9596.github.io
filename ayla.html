Ayla

Ek desktop companion jo mere din ko chalati hai, kyunke mujhe willpower par bharosa nahi.

Mujhe pata hai ke main akeli motivation par nahi chal sakti. Mujhe kuch chahiye jo roz saamne aaye, yaad dilaye, aur din ke end mein poochay ke asal mein kya hua. Toh maine woh khud bana liya, aur woh abhi bhi barh rahi hai.
